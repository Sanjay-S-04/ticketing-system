How the System Works

Every grievance moves through a secure, traceable pipeline with subcollection audit history.

1

Verified Sign-In

Students authenticate securely using institutional @bitsathy.ac.in Google credentials.

2

Automated Mapping

Deterministic keyword analysis recommends the exact root-cause category and target department in real-time.

3

Department Action

Authorized technicians investigate, transition ticket status (Open → In Progress), and post remarks.

4

Resolution & Audit

Issues are resolved with mandatory resolution notes and archived in immutable timeline subcollections.
